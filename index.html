The
Studio

Good houses are quiet. They don't need to announce themselves — they just work, for decades, for the people living in them.

Linea was founded to design homes that respond directly to their site and their owners, rather than to trend. Every project starts from the same three questions: how does light move through this site, how will this family actually use each room, and what will this house need to be for whoever owns it in twenty years.

Process

The same five stages, from first sketch to handover, on every house we design.

01

Brief

We start by understanding the site, the budget, and how you actually want to live in the space, before a single line is drawn.

02

Concept

Early sketches and massing studies test a handful of directions against the site's light, wind and views.

03

Documentation

The chosen concept becomes a full construction set, coordinated with the structural and services engineers.

04

Approval

We manage the planning and building consent process, including any variations or conditions along the way.

05

Construction

We stay involved through the build, working alongside the builder to keep the design intact as it's constructed.
