The
Studio

Good houses are quiet. They don't need to announce themselves — they just work, for decades, for the people living in them.

Linea was founded to design homes that respond directly to their site and their owners, rather than to trend. Every project starts from the same three questions: how does light move through this site, how will this family actually use each room, and what will this house need to be for whoever owns it in twenty years.
